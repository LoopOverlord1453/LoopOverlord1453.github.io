Hi, I'm {{ me.name }}

I'm an embedded software engineer. My interests sit where three fields meet: the software that runs on the board, the signals that come off its sensors, and the control loops that act on them.

I like owning a problem end to end: writing the sensor driver, filtering the data, estimating the state, designing the controller, and turning it into software that runs in real time. When the engineering tool I need does not exist, I build it. That is how Signal Processing Studio and UML Design Studio came about.

Closed Loop Embedded is my public notebook. A closed loop measures its own output and corrects itself, and that is how I try to learn: build something, measure what actually happened, write down the error, fix it.

Hedefim

Hedefim, gömülü Linux'u platform olarak kullanan bir güdüm, navigasyon ve kontrol mühendisi olarak çalışmak. Bu sitedeki projeler o yöndeki adımlar: kestirici ve kontrolcü tasarlamak için bir araç, quadcopter uçuş yazılımı, uydusuz çalışan bir navigasyon sistemi ve bunları gerçek donanıma taşımak için gereken alt düzey Linux becerileri.

Masamdaki kartlar

  • BeagleBone Green (TI AM335x): gömülü Linux
  • STM32N6 geliştirme kiti: uç yapay zekâ
  • ZedBoard (Zynq-7020): FPGA çalışmaları
  • STM32F407 kartı: gömülü yazılım projeleri

Where I am heading

My goal is to work as a guidance, navigation, and control engineer, with embedded Linux as the platform underneath. The projects on this site are steps in that direction: a tool for designing estimators and controllers, flight software for a quadcopter, a navigation system that works without satellites, and the low-level Linux skills to put them on real hardware.
